# Germany channel performance (snapshot 18 Nov 2026)

Source: Fospha Pro Performance Marketing dashboard, "Channel Daily" and "Channel Weekly" tabs, Germany filter.
The tables exclude some campaigns via the "Campaign Type Flag" filter.

## How to read these tables

- **The metrics that count are spend and DDA+ CAC.** DDA+ is Fospha's click-based attribution model, and it's the one Huel follows. FA (Fospha's other attribution model) isn't trusted, so ignore the FA columns.
- **Blended PM CPA is a different number.** It is PM spend divided by *all* new customers, organic included, which is what the Actuals vs Target tab shows. DDA+ CAC is not meant to match it. Huel wants paid media to reach colder audiences, so the aim is the right DDA+ efficiency level for each channel, not a copy of the blended target.
- In Germany, DDA+ credits paid clicks with about half of all new customers: roughly 56 a day in DDA+ against roughly 108 a day in total in November.

## Observations

- **Google non-brand spend was cut by about 47% in the week of 9 Nov** (£10.7k down to £5.6k). New customers fell 32% (91 down to 62) and CAC improved 23% (£117 down to £91). At about £760 a day, the current week is running at the same lower level.
- **The cut spend was buying new customers at about £174 each** (marginal DDA+ CAC: £5.1k ÷ 29 NC), against an average of about £117. That is the price of the extra volume. This is the number to weigh when deciding whether to chase new customers in Germany.
- **On DDA+, Google non-brand is at least as efficient as Meta.** Over 4 weeks both are about £109. In the last 7 days, non-brand was £104 and Meta £119, so Meta has got worse recently. Meta is about 75% of Germany's paid spend.
- **Shopping beats Search every week.** Over 4 weeks Shopping was £97 and Search £127. Search had a weak patch in the weeks of 26 Oct and 2 Nov (£141 to £156).
- **Brand new customers are running about 12% below the 4-week average** (177 in the last 7 days against about 201 a week). That suggests softer demand.
- **Germany is about 252 new customers behind target and about 6% under planned spend**, which fits the non-brand cut. With fewer DDA+ new customers each day, fewer blended new customers follow.
