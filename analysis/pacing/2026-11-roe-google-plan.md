# ROE Google Ads plan (19 Nov 2026)

Account Huel EU (8046071519), EUR. Fospha figures in GBP. Stress-tested with the Opus reviewer on the same data.

**Context**
- Nov MTD: 3,830 new customers (NC), 108% of target. CPA £38.43 against a £33.86 target (113.5%). That makes ROE a slight-trim market.
- Fospha 5-week CAC: PPC generic £168 (£197–200 in the last 2 weeks), Shopping £104, Brand £10, Meta £159 (77% of spend), TikTok £171.
- NC below = Google "New customer Purchases".

## Actions
1. **ROE_SEARCH_BRAND_EM: raise the budget from €400 to €600 today (Manual CPC, a one-off change).**
   - **Why:** the budget is already limiting spend. Budget-lost IS was 10%, 37% and 5% on 16–18 Nov, with spend pinned at €399–401 a day.
   - The monthly limit is €12,160 and MTD spend is €8,002, which leaves €346 a day against a €445 run rate. Without the raise, Google throttles harder and stops serving around 28 Nov, during BFCM.
   - **Volume:** about 235–297 NC a week at about €12 each. Losing 18% of IS puts about 40 NC a week at risk now.
   - **Why €600:** the new limit is spend to date + €600 × days left. That allows about €600 a day: the €445 run rate plus BFCM.
2. **Reactivate IT_SEARCH_GENERIC as it was (tCPA €110, €400 budget) and exclude Italy from ROE_SEARCH_GENERIC.**
   - **Why:** since IT was folded into ROE on 3 Nov, Italy has dropped from about €2.7k for 13.6 NC a week to €0.8k for 3.7 NC a week. ROE generic targets en/fr/es/nl only and has no Italian keywords, so "sostituto pasto" (IT's top term, €3.2k for 24 conversions) can't match.
   - **Combined Search:** before 3 Nov, €7.8k bought 46 NC a week (€168 each). Since then, €8.4k buys 42 NC a week (€201 each).
   - **Expected:** +9–10 NC a week for about +€1.9k a week.
3. **ROE_SEARCH_GENERIC: cut the budget from €920 back to €750,** where it was before the consolidation.
   - **Why:** outside Italy, the 3 Nov raise bought +€2.3k a week for +5.1 NC, about €446 per extra NC (about €325 after adjusting for seasonality). The 30 Oct tCPA tightening should have helped over the same period, so the true marginal is, if anything, worse. At €920, NC is sliding at flat spend: 44.7 → 35.3 → about 31.5 a week, at €222 each.
   - **Expected:** −€2.1k a week, −5 NC a week.
4. **ROE_SEARCH_GENERIC: pause the weakest items.** The campaign is capped, so the freed spend moves to Meal Replacement (€161 per NC) and Protein (€164).
   - **Pause these competitor keywords** (exact and broad): Bulk protein (€539 / 0 NC), ESN (€405 / 0.5), Ag1 (€471 / 1), prepmymeal exact (€365 / 0), factor meals (€973 / 3). As a group: €2.75k for 4.5 NC, against about 14.5 expected at the campaign rate.
   - **Keep:** jimmy joy (€1,278 / 9) and yfood (€2,288 / 9). Without the losers, the Competitors ad group runs at €180 per NC.
   - **Pause the Greens ad group** (€1,686 / 5 NC, €337 each) and **the Protein Bars ad group** (€881 / 3, €294).
   - **Exclude Switzerland:** €1,097 for 2 conversions since Aug, and 0 in Oct–Nov.
   - **Expected:** about €830 a week reallocated, for a net +2–3 NC.
5. **ROE_SHOPPING_STN_GENERIC: lower tROAS from 102% to 90% now, and keep it through BFCM.** For 27–30 Nov, apply the BFCM AOV factor to the 90% base.
   - **Why:** ROAS has stayed below target (0.90–0.92) for weeks, so Google cut CPC from €1.70 to €0.98. Daily spend fell from about €400 (1–10 Nov) to €150–260 (11–18 Nov). The earlier cut from 108% to 102% did nothing, because realised ROAS stayed below the new target as well.
   - **Cost per NC has stayed flat at €84–116** at every spend level from €1.9k to €3.2k a week. CVR is intact (1.55% → 1.46%), and there was no 16–18 Nov collapse like DE/PL.
   - **Expected:** +€0.8–1.6k a week and +6–12 NC a week.
   - **Kill-switch:** revert to 102% if cost per NC over 14 days (by conversion date) is above €140, or Fospha Shopping CAC is above £130 for a week. A 7-day read is ±45% noise at about 20 NC a week.
6. **Note: Denmark's NC tag isn't firing.** DK has 42 conversions since Aug and 0 NC, so NC-based reads on DK are blind.

**Net:** Search and Shopping together are about +€0.6–1.4k a week for +12–20 NC a week, and the cost per NC falls. The brand fix recovers about 40 NC a week at about €12 each. Blended ROE CPA moves down. The rest of the CPA overage is a Meta question.
