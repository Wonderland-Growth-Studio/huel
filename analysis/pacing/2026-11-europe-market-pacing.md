# Europe market pacing: November 2026 (snapshot 18 Nov)

Source: Fospha Pro Performance Marketing dashboard (ThoughtSpot), "Actuals vs Target" tab.
Figures are Fospha-attributed paid media (PM) numbers, not platform-reported.
Data runs to about 17 Nov. "Expected New Customers" is the full-month NC target.

## How Huel makes the call

1. Read NC pacing (actual vs target to date) and CPA pacing (PM CPA vs target) for each region and market.
2. Classify the market:
   - NC behind, CPA on target: chase NC, add budget.
   - NC on track, CPA over: save budget, trim.
   - NC behind, CPA over: reallocate to efficient campaigns, don't just scale.
3. Use channel-level efficiency to decide where to add or cut.
4. Decide actions per channel.

Regions: UK, US, Europe. Michael owns Google Ads for Europe and the UK.

## Observations

- **Rest of Europe** is the only market ahead on NC, and its +294 hides the Germany, Poland and Sweden shortfall in the Europe total. It is spending about 22% over plan to get there, so trimming fits.
- **Germany** is the furthest behind on NC (−252) but is *under* planned spend, with CPA only 7% over target. It looks more like a volume and budget problem than an efficiency problem. It is the best candidate to chase NC.
- **Sweden** is the least efficient market (CPA 131% of target) and is already overspent against plan, so more budget isn't converting into NC. It needs efficiency fixes before scaling.
- **Poland** spend is on plan, but CPA is 14% over and NC 10% behind. It is a mixed case; check which channels are driving the CPA.
- In the daily charts, CPA has been above target on most days since mid-October in every market. Poland and Sweden have had some green days in late October and early November.
