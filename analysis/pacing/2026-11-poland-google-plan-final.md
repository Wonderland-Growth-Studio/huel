# Poland Google Ads: final plan (19 Nov 2026)

Account Huel EU (8046071519), EUR; Fospha figures are GBP. I drafted from the data. An Opus reviewer checked the same data independently, then challenged the draft. I then verified the disputed points myself.

**Context**
- **Poland Nov MTD:** 89.8% of the new-customer target to date. CPA £24.61 against a £21.50 target (114.5%).
- **Fospha 4-week CAC:**
  - Google Shopping £119 (weekly £113–123).
  - Meta £117 (£140–162 at £7.8–8.7k a week, £91–94 at £5.9–6.4k a week).
  - Google Search generic £158.
  - Google Brand £2.68.

## Actions we are taking

1. **Diagnose the generic Shopping conversion drop today (Poland and Germany). Decide on 21 Nov.**
   - **The drop is real, not lag.** By conversion date, PL generic Shopping fell from 7.1 to 2.9 conversions a day (16–18 Nov against 9–15 Nov) on the same clicks. Conversion rate went from 1.24% to 0.54%.
   - **Germany shows the same pattern:** 8.8 to 3.6 a day, conversion rate from 1.9% to 1.0%.
   - **It's not tracking.** Brand Shopping on the same days is normal in Poland (7.2 to 8.0 a day) and only mildly down in Germany. Rest of Europe and Sweden generic Shopping did not drop (ROE 3.4 a day in both periods).
   - **What to check:**
     - Merchant Center: price and availability changes.
     - Any Black Week pre-announcement, which would make new customers wait.
     - Product pages for the top SKUs.
   - **Don't lower any Shopping target into this.** tROAS bidding will throttle on its own while conversion rate is down.

2. **Pause "posiłek w proszku" (BROAD) in PL_SEARCH_GENERIC.**
   - **Why:** €348 spent with 0 conversions. At the campaign's ~€105–114 CPA, the chance of zero is about 5%.

3. **Keep PL_SEARCH_GENERIC tROAS at 70%** (Huel's change on 16 Nov).
   - **Why:** Search generic is Poland's most expensive line in Fospha: £158 over 4 weeks, against £117–119 for Meta and Shopping.

4. **PL_SEARCH_BRAND: check auction insights in the UI.**
   - **Why:** IS fell from 86% to 71–80% in November, rank-lost IS rose from 13–15% to 20–29%, and CPC went from €0.098 to €0.113. That pattern fits a new competitor bidding on Huel terms (for example on "huel opinie", €0.39 CPC).

## Actions we are not taking

5. **Not pausing the Competitors ad group.**
   - **Why:** I proposed pausing it on 4 weeks of data (€228 CPA), but over Aug–Nov it ran at €3.9k for 37 conversions, a €106 CPA. That's the same as the campaign average of about €105. The bad stretch is recent, and the 70% tROAS already throttles weaker ad groups.

6. **Not switching "białko roślinne" to phrase match.**
   - **Why:** in the search terms, the broad expansions (e.g. "bialko bez laktozy") are about 85% of its volume, at a €134 CPA. The core "roślinne" queries run at €155. Phrase would cut the cheaper part. Over 16 weeks the keyword is €7.4k for 72 conversions, a €103 CPA, which is average.

7. **No PL Shopping tROAS (70%) or budget (€850) change.**
   - **Why:** Fospha CAC was steady at £113–123 for 4 weeks, and the budget isn't limiting spend (0% lost since September). The recent drop is a conversion-rate problem (action 1), not a bid problem.

8. **PL brand stays on Target Impression Share** (75% anywhere on page, €0.50 cap).
   - **Why:** it isn't conversion-based bidding. "huel" exact runs at €0.02 CPC with 96% IS. Total spend is about €300 a week, so manual CPC could save €60 a week at most.

9. **No Black Friday changes in Poland.**
   - **Why:** in Black Friday week 2025, Polish brand and Shopping showed no lift. Brand conversions were flat at about 160–235 a week, with 0% lost to budget.

10. **Not recommending a move of money from Google to Meta.**
    - **Why:** on the same 4 weeks Meta (£117) and Shopping (£119) cost the same. Meta's £91–94 weeks came at lower spend (£5.9–6.4k against £7.8–8.7k), so more money is likely to push Meta back toward £140+.
